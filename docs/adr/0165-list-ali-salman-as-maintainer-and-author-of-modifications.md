RC MetaStudio package metadata should identify Ali Salman and Research Consultancy (RC) as the current maintainer/publisher identity and Ali Salman as author of modifications where metadata fields need a current author. RCMetaR `Authors@R` may list Ali Salman as `aut` and `cre` for the maintained private package while preserving original authorship context outside the active maintainer fields.
