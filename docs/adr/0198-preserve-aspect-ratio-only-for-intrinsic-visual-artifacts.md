# Preserve Aspect Ratio Only for Intrinsic Visual Artifacts

RC MetaStudio will preserve aspect ratio for Intrinsic-Ratio Artifacts such as plots, diagrams, and raster or vector previews, allowing unused viewport space rather than distorting them. Network diagrams should refit or regenerate for their viewport, while top-level windows, forms, tables, and general panes remain freely resizable and must not impose aspect-ratio constraints on the user.
