# Prioritize Random Effects in the Initial Golden Methods

The initial curated golden set should prioritize random-effects methods for each data family, plus at least one cumulative or leave-one-out random-effects case. Random-effects paths exercise heterogeneity-related outputs and represent central meta-analysis behavior, making them a better first compatibility signal than a large spread of fixed-effect variants.

Fixed-effect methods should be added after the capture and comparison pipeline is stable.
